# Analysis plan and reporting decisions

## Design

Course-embedded, single-group, three-wave mixed-methods study. T0, T1, and T2 were treated as successive stages: intuitive human-centred understanding before the concept-focused lecture, environmental psychology learning immediately after Lecture 1, and spatial analysis after students used the concepts to examine a real interior. The design supports within-person description and association, not causal attribution or a comparison of instructional methods.

## Quantitative outcomes

The primary self-report outcome was the equally weighted mean of eight 1-5 items. Internal consistency was reported with Cronbach's alpha. Overall stage differences used the Friedman test. Pairwise contrasts used Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm adjustment within each outcome. Rank-biserial correlation described paired effect size.

Item-level analyses were exploratory. Baseline scores of complete and incomplete longitudinal cases were compared with the Mann-Whitney U test. The effect of retaining the latest duplicate response was examined with an earliest-response sensitivity analysis.

## Assignment evidence

One researcher re-reviewed 44 submissions using five dimensions scored from 0 to 3:

1. human-space relationship;
2. psychological needs;
3. multisensory experience, arousal, and adaptation;
4. affordances; and
5. spatial Gestalt.

The total ranged from 0 to 15. Scores were kept separate from course grades and interpreted as descriptive supporting evidence. High-detail work was defined for exploratory analysis as a total of at least 13 with no dimension below 2. This was an analytical threshold, not a validated mastery standard.

Public figures report aggregate estimates only. Individual response trajectories and small-cell studio distributions are not included in the public repository.

Spearman correlations examined assignment totals in relation to T2 self-report, T1-T2 change, and prior studio progression. Studio correlations for the total and five dimensions were Holm-adjusted as one exploratory family. High-detail and other scored assignments were compared on T1-T2 change with the Mann-Whitney U test and Cliff's delta.

## Open-ended indicators

Open-ended responses were summarized with concept-guided, keyword-assisted, multi-label indicators. The procedure mapped concept visibility across stages and was not treated as an independent, fully interpretive thematic analysis. No verbatim student response is included in the public repository.

## Missingness and privacy

The primary longitudinal tests used the 34 complete cases. Missing values were not imputed. Students without a matched assignment were not labelled as low-performing. Direct identifiers and verbatim responses are excluded from the public materials.
